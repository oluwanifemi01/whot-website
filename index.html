Rules

How to
play.

01
Deal the cards
Each player gets 6 cards. One card is flipped face-up to start the play pile. The rest form the draw pile.
02
Match suit or number
Play a card that matches the suit or number of the top card. If you can't play, draw one from the pile.
03
Use special cards
Special cards disrupt your opponent — force pickups, skip turns, or call any suit you want with Whot 20.
04
Go out first
Play your last card to win the round. Opponent's remaining hand is counted as penalty points. First to 100 loses.

Special cards

1 Hold On Play again immediately
2 Pick Two Opponent draws 2 (stackable)
5 Pick Three Opponent draws 3 (stackable)
8 Suspend Opponent skips their turn
14 General Market Everyone else draws 1 card
20 Whot Wild — call any suit you want
