Rules

How to
play.

01
Deal the cards
Each player gets 6 cards. One is flipped face-up to start the play pile.
02
Match suit or number
Play a card matching the suit or number of the top card. If you can't, draw one.
03
Use special cards
Force pickups, skip turns, or call any suit with the Whot 20 wild card.
04
Go out first
Play your last card to win. Opponent's hand scores penalty points. First to 100 loses.
